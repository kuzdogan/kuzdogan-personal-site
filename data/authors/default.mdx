- Born and raised in [Kayseri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kayseri), in central Turkey.
- Went to high school in Istanbul, at [Istanbul Erkek Lisesi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istanbul_High_School).
- Studied computer science at [Boğaziçi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bo%C4%9Fazi%C3%A7i_University), also in Istanbul.
- Heard about Bitcoin, then Ethereum, near the end of my bachelor's. Traded a bit first, then wanted to know how it works. My final project was [a voting system on Ethereum](https://seniorprojects.cmpe.bogazici.edu.tr/semesters/2017-fall/ebloc-blockchain-voting/), in the early days of Solidity.
- Moved to Munich for a master's in Informatics at the [Technical University of Munich](https://www.tum.de). Took the [blockchain course](https://github.com/sebischair/bbse) there, and later taught it as a teaching assistant.
- Worked part-time at the Max Planck Society on [bloxberg](https://bloxberg.org), an Ethereum chain run by research institutes. My first time running EVM nodes.
- Wrote my thesis on [peer review verification with verifiable credentials and zero-knowledge proofs](https://github.com/kuzdogan/peer-review-verifiable-credentials-thesis).
- In September 2021, joined the Ethereum Foundation as the only developer on [Sourcify](https://sourcify.dev). Moved to Berlin.
- Marco joined in 2022, and Manuel in 2024. We are three since then.
- Helped with [Devconnect Istanbul](https://devconnect.org/istanbul) in 2023, and wrote a [food guide](/blog/older/devconnect-food) for it. Two years later, [another one](/blog/2025/bbw-2025-foodie-guide) for Berlin Blockchain Week.
- In 2025, Sourcify left the Ethereum Foundation with the Solidity team and others, to form the [Argot Collective](https://argot.org).
- In 2026, moved back to Istanbul. Sourcify became a founding member of the Clear Signing Working Group. Spoke about it at Devcon 8: [Goodbye YOLO Signing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EHU2mXIWgeo).
- Outside work, I [cook](/blog/2024/cooking-matters), [run](https://www.strava.com/athletes/31545219), and enjoy snowboarding. Coming to Istanbul? [Ask me](https://x.com/kaanuzdogan) where to eat.
